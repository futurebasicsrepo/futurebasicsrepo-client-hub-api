Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you take walk-ins?
Yes, Monday to Saturday from 9:30 am. Join the waitlist above and we'll text you when your chair is nearly ready, or book ahead to skip it entirely.
How long does gel or dip last?
Gel usually lasts two to three weeks and SNS dip three to four. Acrylics need a fill every two to three weeks.
Do you charge for removal?
Gel removal is $5 with a new service ($15 without), SNS removal $5 ($20 without), and acrylic soak-off is $25.
Do you do kids' nails?
Yes, for 10 and under: polish change $15, manicure $20, gel $20–25 and pedicure $28.
Is the wine really free?
It is. Wine, beer and soft drinks are complimentary with any service (alcohol for guests 21+).
Can you host a bridal party or birthday?
We'd love to. Tell us your date and group size and we'll seat you side by side. Plan a group visit.
