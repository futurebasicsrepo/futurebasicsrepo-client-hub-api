Re-engagement for applicants who go quiet

Most applicants who stop replying aren't uninterested; they're working a double. Three gentle texts, then we stop.

  1. After 1 day: "Hi {first}, still interested in caring with NursePartners? Tap to pick up where you left off: {link}"
  2. After 3 days: "We have clients near {area} who need someone {availability}. Want to grab a 15-min call? Reply a time."
  3. After 7 days: "Last note from us. If now's not the time, reply LATER and we'll check in next month."

Human in the loop, by design

  • Screening questions are job-related only: certification, experience, area, availability, transport
  • No criminal-history or salary-history questions before a conditional offer (Philadelphia Fair Chance and wage equity rules)
  • Low experience never auto-rejects; it's flagged for Robert
  • Every status change is logged with who made it

    Guardrails

    Built for an agency near PHI

    The recruiting side is clean. The family side touches health information, so it's scoped tightly from day one.

    No PHI in plain text

    SMS and email carry times and links only. Care Line chat tells families not to share medical details and flags if they do. Clinical detail lives behind an authenticated portal.

    BAAs with every vendor

    Only HIPAA-eligible services that sign a Business Associate Agreement touch visit notes or referrals: hosting, SMS, email, AI drafting. No model training on agency data.

    Consent, logged

    TCPA-compliant text opt-in on every form, STOP honored instantly, and family-update recipients authorized in writing by the client or POA.

    People make hiring decisions

    Automation schedules and reminds. It doesn't score or reject. Questions stay job-related; criminal and salary history wait until a conditional offer, as Philadelphia requires.

    Nurse approves clinical content

    Family digests are drafts until a nurse approves them. Any mention of pain, falls, skin, appetite change or confusion becomes a clinical task, not just a sentence.

    Audit trail

    Who saw what and who sent what, kept for six years. Exportable for a Department of Health survey.
