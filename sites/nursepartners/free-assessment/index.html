Free · in-home · no obligation

Meet a nurse at the kitchen table.

About an hour, at home or at the hospital before discharge. Our nurse gets to know your person, checks the home for safety, and writes a care plan with you.

You'll leave with

  • A written care plan: tasks, hours and goals
  • A clear monthly cost, in writing
  • Help starting an LTC insurance claim, if there's a policy
  • A GEMS dementia assessment, where it applies
  • A start date, often the next day
We won't ask for medical details here. Diagnoses, medications and history are discussed privately with the nurse in person. This form only collects what we need to schedule.
