Questions families ask

Straight answers.

Does Medicare pay for home care?
No. Medicare covers short-term, skilled home health after a hospital stay, not ongoing help with bathing, meals, mobility or companionship. NursePartners is licensed by the Pennsylvania Department of Health as a home care agency. Families typically pay privately, with long-term care insurance, or with VA benefits. See every option.
Do you work with long-term care insurance?
Yes. We accept assignment of benefits, so the insurer pays us directly. We help you open the claim and handle the monthly paperwork, and bill you only for any balance the policy doesn't cover.
Is there a minimum number of hours?
Yes: 4 hours a visit, twice a week, in Philadelphia County and in Delaware and Montgomery Counties east of I-476. Elsewhere in Chester, Delaware and Montgomery Counties it's 6 hours, twice a week.
How quickly can care start?
The nurse assessment can usually happen within 24 hours. Our goal is to have a carepartner in the home within 24 hours of that, often the same day as the referral.
Will we have the same caregiver?
That's the goal. We introduce a primary and a relief carepartner from the start, so if your primary is sick, someone your loved one already knows covers the shift.
What can a carepartner do?
Bathing and grooming, toileting and incontinence care, mobility and transfers, feeding and diet monitoring, meal preparation, light housekeeping, laundry, errands, transportation to appointments, companionship and medication reminders. Carepartners can open containers and remind, but they don't administer medication.
Who answers after hours?
A member of our leadership team with clinical experience is on call 24/7, 365 days a year, at (610) 323-9800.
