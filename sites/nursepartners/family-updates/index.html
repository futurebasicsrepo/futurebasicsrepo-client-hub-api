What happens in between

  1. Drafted automatically from the week's notes: jargon ("RW", "c/o", "pt") becomes plain English, and anything the family needs to act on moves to the top.
  2. Flags for the nurse: the knee pain went to the nurse as a clinical follow-up, not just a line in the email.
  3. A nurse reviews and approves every update before it's sent. Nothing goes to a family unread.
  4. Sent Friday at 4pm to the family members the client (or power of attorney) has authorized.
Why it matters: families who hear from the agency every week stay longer, add hours sooner, and are the ones who leave reviews.

Consent first

Updates go only to people the client, or their power of attorney, names in writing. They can add or remove anyone at any time.

No health details by text

Texts and emails say only "this week's update is ready" with a link. The update itself sits behind a secure, expiring login.

Covered vendors only

Visit notes and drafts are processed only by services that sign a Business Associate Agreement, and are never used to train anyone's models.
