Rowhome-ready care

Steep stairs, one upstairs bathroom, front steps. We plan for all of it.

Most South Philly homes weren't built for aging in place. At the free assessment our nurse walks the house with you: the stoop, the stairs, the only bathroom on the second floor. Then we build a plan that keeps your parent safe on them, or shows you where a grab bar, stair lift or bedroom move downstairs would help most.

Our carepartners are trained in safe transfers and fall prevention, and they know the reality of parking on a one-way street at 7am.

South Philly FAQ

Local questions, local answers.

Do you serve all of South Philadelphia?
Yes: ZIP codes 19145, 19146, 19147, 19148 and 19112, from Queen Village and Bella Vista down to Packer Park and the Navy Yard. The minimum is 4 hours a visit, twice a week.
What does home care cost in South Philadelphia?
Home care is billed hourly. For reference, the 2025 Pennsylvania median for non-medical in-home care was $34 an hour (CareScout). You'll get an exact written quote at the free assessment. Estimate your schedule.
Can care start right after a hospital discharge?
Yes. A nurse can meet you at the hospital or rehab before discharge so a carepartner is ready on the first day home.
Can you help with stairs in a rowhome?
Yes. Our nurse checks stairs, the bathroom and entry steps at the assessment, and carepartners are trained in safe transfers and fall prevention.
Can my mother keep her own routine, like Sunday Mass or the senior center?
Absolutely. Carepartners can drive or accompany her to appointments, Mass, the senior center or the market. Routine is part of the care plan.
