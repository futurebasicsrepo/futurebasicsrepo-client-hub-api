Dementia & Alzheimer's care at home

We let go of what's missing and build on what remains.

Dementia care is where NursePartners began. Every plan is built by a nurse using Teepa Snow's GEMS® model, and every carepartner is trained in the Positive Approach® to Care, so help feels like partnership, not correction.

The GEMS model

Six gems, one person.

GEMS describes how someone experiences the world as the brain changes. People move between states, even within a day. Tap a gem to see what it means and how we help.

Tap a gem

After the in-home assessment our nurse writes activity lists and communication techniques for your person's current state, and updates them as things change.

GEMS® and Positive Approach® to Care are trademarks of Positive Approach, LLC (Teepa Snow).

Led by a dementia practitioner

Founder Angela Geiger RN is a nationally credentialed dementia practitioner with 30+ years in geriatric care. Our clinical coordinator is a certified PAC Independent Champion Teacher.

Trained every year

Every carepartner completes paid dementia training when hired and again every year, including hand-under-hand technique and approaching without startling.

Support for you, too

Respite hours for family caregivers, tips in the weekly update, and coaching on what to say when your mom asks for her own mother.
