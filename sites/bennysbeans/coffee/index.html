    Good to know.

    When will it be roasted?

    On the next Monday or Wednesday after you order (orders close at 11:59 pm the night before). It ships the following day, so it usually arrives two to four days off the roaster.

    Not sure which roast?

    Choose Roaster's Choice. Benny roasts each batch to the level he thinks shows that bean best, based on how it roasts. The gold dots mark the range he recommends for this coffee.

    Whole bean or ground?

    Whole bean keeps its flavor longest. If you don't grind at home, pick the grind for your brewer; Benny's grinder runs at a low RPM so the grind doesn't heat and flatten the coffee.

    Shipping and pickup

    Free shipping on orders of $60 or more. Will-call pickup at True Value Hardware in Guerneville and farmers-market pickup are always free. Questions? Text Benny at 707-899-4183.
