Coffees you're interested in

Wholesale questions.

Is there a minimum order?

Wholesale pricing in the calculator starts at 5 lb a week (illustrative). Smaller than that? The 5 lb bag at $80 is already $16 a pound, or ask Benny; he works with small accounts.

Can you grind for us?

Yes: Coarse, Auto-Drip, Espresso, Fine or Turkish, on a grinder modified to run at a much lower RPM so heat doesn't flatten the flavor. Whole bean keeps longest if you have a grinder.

Do you provide equipment?

Ask Benny. Equipment depends on the account and isn't part of the standard program, so talk it through before you plan around it.

Can we lock in a roast level?

That's the point of a standing order. Choose Light through French, or Roaster's Choice, and Benny keeps your profile consistent from batch to batch.

How does billing work?

The plan is one invoice per month for everything roasted that month (illustrative). Benny confirms terms when he sends your quote.

We're a vacation rental with two units. Is that enough?

Yes. The welcome-bag program is built for small hosts. Tell Benny how many check-ins you get a month and he'll suggest a bag size and a delivery rhythm.
