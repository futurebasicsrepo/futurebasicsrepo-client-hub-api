        Subscription questions.

        What's in a rotation?

        Benny's pick is whatever he's most excited about that roast day, roasted at Roaster's Choice. The Light rotation moves through brighter single origins like the Ethiopia Buture, roasted light. The Dark rotation leans on bold, low-acid beans like the Mysore Nuggets and Coorg Robusta, roasted dark. Prefer one coffee every time? Lock it in.

        How do skip, swap and pause work?

        The day before each roast you get a text. Reply SKIP to skip that delivery, SWAP to change coffee, PAUSE to stop until you say so. Or use the link in the text. Changes count any time before roast day.

        When does my coffee get roasted?

        On the Monday or Wednesday before each delivery, then it ships the next day. Your first bag goes on the next roast day after you sign up.

        Is shipping free?

        Free on any delivery of $60 or more, so the 5 lb plan always ships free. Smaller deliveries pay shipping at checkout, or switch to free will-call pickup at True Value Hardware in Guerneville or the Occidental and Cloverdale farmers markets.

        Can I cancel?

        Any time, by text or link. No minimums, no fees.

        How much coffee do I need?

        A typical cup uses about 15–16 g of beans, so a pound makes roughly 28–30 cups. Two cups a day is about a pound every two weeks. The helper in step three does the math for you.
