Good questions.

How fresh is "fresh"?

Benny doesn't keep roasted coffee on hand. Your bag is roasted on the next Monday or Wednesday after you order and ships the following day, so it usually arrives two to four days off the roaster.

What's Roaster's Choice?

Instead of choosing Light through French yourself, you let Benny roast each batch to the level he thinks shows that bean best. It's new, and the easiest way to taste a coffee the way the roaster intends.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean keeps its flavor longest. If you don't grind at home, choose Coarse (French press, cold brew), Auto-Drip, Espresso, Fine or Turkish. Benny's grinder runs at a low RPM so the grind doesn't heat and flatten the coffee.

How much is shipping?

Free on orders of $60 or more. Under that, shipping is calculated at checkout. Will-call pickup at True Value Hardware in Guerneville and market pickup are always free.

Can I talk to Benny?

Yes, really. Text 707-899-4183. He's happy to talk coffee, roast levels and wholesale.
