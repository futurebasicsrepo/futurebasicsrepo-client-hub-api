Gift questions.

How is a gift card delivered?

By email, to the address you enter, either right away or on the date you schedule. It includes your note, the design you picked and a redeem code.

Do gift cards expire?

No. They never expire (illustrative policy; Benny confirms the final terms).

Where can they redeem it?

Online at checkout, or in person at the Benny's Beans booth at the Occidental Farmers Market (Thursdays 4–8 pm) and the Cloverdale Certified Farmers Market (Sundays 9:30 am–1 pm).

When does a gift subscription start?

On the first roast day of the month you choose. Each month's bag is roasted to order on a Monday or Wednesday and ships the next day.

Can I ship a gift bag straight to them?

Yes. Add the coffee, then enter their address at checkout. Orders of $60 or more ship free.
