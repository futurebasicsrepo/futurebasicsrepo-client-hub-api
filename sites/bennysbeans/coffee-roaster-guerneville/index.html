Local questions.

Where can I buy fresh roasted coffee in Guerneville?

Order online and choose will-call pickup at True Value Hardware of Guerneville. Your bag roasts on the next Monday or Wednesday and is ready the day after.

When does Benny roast?

Every Monday and Wednesday. Order now and it roasts . Shipped orders go out the next day; free shipping on $60+.

Is there a café I can visit?

Not a café: Benny's Beans is a roastery that sells online, at will-call and at the Occidental and Cloverdale farmers markets. The best place to taste and talk coffee with Benny is the market booth.

Do you sell to cafés, restaurants and rentals?

Yes. Wholesale pricing for cafés, restaurants, offices and vacation rentals, with standing orders on the Monday or Wednesday roast.
