    Pickup questions.

    Do I need to pre-order for the market?

    No, but it's the only way to guarantee the coffee, roast and grind you want. Benny roasts in small batches to order, so walk-up stock is whatever's left from that week's roast.

    How do I pay for a pre-order?

    At the booth when you pick up. Nothing is charged online. Give your name or pickup code and your bags are handed over.

    When is will-call ready?

    The day after roasting: Tuesday for Monday roasts, Thursday for Wednesday roasts. Order online, choose will-call at checkout, and you'll get a text when it's on the shelf (illustrative).

    What if a market is cancelled?

    Weather and market seasons change. If a market is off, Benny texts everyone with a pre-order and moves it to the next market or to will-call, whichever you prefer.

    Can I pre-order 5 lb bags?

    Yes. Every coffee comes in 1 lb ($20) or 5 lb ($80), any roast, any grind.
