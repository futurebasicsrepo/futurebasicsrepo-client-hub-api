What's moving

The library read as numbers: prices by category, how much of each is selling, and the colours, fabrics, shapes and details that keep showing up. Our ladder is the price range a piece in that category is held to: Zara's own prices where the Lab has read them, otherwise an estimate from the reference brands.

Line plans

An assortment for a season: which pieces, in which role, at what price, with an estimated landed cost and a starting quantity. The assistant builds a first draft from the library and the brand code; you change anything. Landed cost is a share of the shelf price until a concept becomes a real tech pack, where the bill of materials gives the true number.

Concepts

Our own take on each planned piece: a picture, a specific twist, and a check against what inspired it. Look at a concept beside the pieces it was drawn from before you approve it. An approved concept becomes a product in the Future Basics room and its tech pack is drafted from it.

Assortment board

Every concept in one visual wall: laid flat and on a model. Open one to turn the model all the way round, or, with a video model connected, watch the model walk in the piece. Pictures are drawn from each concept's own picture, so the shape, colours, logo and graphics stay the ones you reviewed.

Brands we watch

The Lab reads each one on its own every few days. It follows each site's robots.txt, goes slowly, and when a site says no, or sits behind a bot wall, it finds the brand's pieces through ordinary web and image search instead.

What the Lab has read

    The Lab keeps facts and links, and the address of a picture on the brand's own site. It does not copy pictures, logos, prints or names into our work. Every concept later is checked against what it was inspired by before anyone can approve it.
