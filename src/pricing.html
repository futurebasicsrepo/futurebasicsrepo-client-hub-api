Questions

Before you start

Is the first pack really free?

Yes. Your first pack is free, with no card. If the assistant cannot read your photo, that does not use it up: we ask for a better picture.

What do I get for $48 that I do not get free?

The same pack, for every product after the first. The price is per pack, so a product with a few revisions is still one pack.

Do the packs go to a factory for me?

You can download the pack and send it to any factory yourself. If you would rather we send it to the right factories, compare their quotes and run samples, that is a project with us.

Can I open it in Excel?

Yes. Every pack downloads as one workbook, or as CSV for any sheet. When we send a pack to a factory, your retail price and your costs are left out.

Is the cost estimate a quote?

No. It is your own arithmetic: unit costs you enter, added up with freight and duty. A factory's quote replaces it.
