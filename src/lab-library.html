Put it on a concept

The graphic is added to the concept and placed on its picture. Open the concept or the studio to finish it.

Factory job file

A zip for the factory or digitiser: the artwork at 300 dpi, one layer and film per colour with its Pantone, the underbase, halftone and mesh (print) or stitch, thread and backing (embroidery), the cut line (patches), a one-page job sheet and job.json.

Make an all-over repeat

The graphic repeated without a seam, for a sublimated or all-over printed piece. Kept in the library, it can cover a whole piece on its flats.

Put it on a product

Any product, in any client's room: the graphic goes on its tech pack as artwork, placed on the front view. The pack is made first if the product has none yet.

Reference photos of the real thing

Up to four close-ups of the real technique (a thread, an ink, a film). They go to the image model next to the piece whenever it is made real.
