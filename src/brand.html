Future Basics · for the team

Brand and assets.

The wordmark in every colour and file type, icons, social and email images, the colours and the fonts. Everything here is ready to use or download, for web and for print.

Use it like this

  • Vector firstUse the SVG or PDF whenever you can. They never blur. PNG, WebP and JPG are for places that cannot take a vector.
  • Ink on light, white on darkPrint in one colour: black. Cue green only on Ink.
  • Room to breatheLeave a margin as tall as the wordmark on every side. Never smaller than 120 px wide on screen or 30 mm in print.
  • Keep it wholeDo not stretch, skew, outline, add shadows or rearrange it. On a photo, put it on a plain area or an Ink panel.
