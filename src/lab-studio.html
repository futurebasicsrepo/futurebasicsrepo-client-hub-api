How long is that line?

The line is pixels. Say how long it is on the piece, and every layer gets its real size: a chest width, a pocket, a label you know.

Tone on tone

The fabric's own colour and shades of it, a step lighter and darker: the quiet way to put a mark on a piece. Each has its nearest Pantone C chip; have the factory match it to the fabric lab dip.
