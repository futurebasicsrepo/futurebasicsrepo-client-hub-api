# Site prototypes (SW and site prototyping)

`/prototypes` (menu: Sales → Site prototypes). Paste the address of a small business's site and the platform reads it, scores it, builds a new high-standard site for their trade, checks it against a standard, and publishes it onto the Builds showcase to share. Aimed at quick-turn leads in blue-collar trades (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, carpentry and remodeling, landscaping, painting, cleaning) and home health care.

## The flow
1. **Audit (free, no AI).** Fetches the home page and up to three more (services, about, contact, areas) and scores the site out of 100: not built for phones, no https, phone number not tap-to-call, no quote form, no reviews or licence shown, outdated markup, a stale © year, slow, weak title and search description, no local-business data, and so on. Each finding comes with a sentence a rep can say to the owner, and the site is marked **quick turn** (small, no shop or portal) or a bigger job. It also names the trade.
2. **Build.** The brief is made from what the site says (name, phone, services, hours, colours, pictures) plus the trade's playbook (`src/proto-industry.js`): the sections a visitor in that trade expects, the proof that earns trust, the tone, and the interactive features for the trade (an emergency panel, a "what is wrong?" picker, a do-you-serve-me checker, an instant estimate, before and after, a care needs quiz for home health, colour visualizers, and more). Claude (`claude-opus-5-5`, streamed) builds one self-contained HTML page to the standard in `src/proto-build.js` (mobile first, tap-to-call everywhere, accessible, local-search ready, under 450 KB, motion that sets the work apart: animated hero, scroll reveals, count-ups, responsive buttons, all switched off for reduced motion). With no Claude connected it uses a standard template, and says so.
3. **Honesty.** Only facts from their site are shown as facts. Anything else the page needs (sample reviews, ballpark prices, counts) must be marked `data-illustrative` and shows a small "Sample" tag; the page lists them as "confirm with the owner". Their own pictures can be hot-linked and the page says when it does.
4. **Quality gate.** 27 checks, most of them required: doctype, language, viewport, title and description, exactly one heading, landmarks, size, no outside scripts (every inline script must compile), no outside stylesheets, forms that never submit, alt text, labelled fields, reduced-motion, motion, an interactive feature, tap-to-call, the phone number as given, local-business data, no placeholder text. Safe fixes are applied automatically (`autofix`); a failed required check triggers one repair pass; if that still fails the template is used. A page that fails cannot be published.
5. **Publish and share.** Publishing puts the page at `/p/<slug>` (an unguessable address) and creates a **Builds** entry (hidden until shared; sector, summary and highlights filled in). **Share** invites the owner's email to that build (the showcase emails them a sign-in code) or copies the link. Taking it down ends the link at once and hides the build; deleting archives the build.

## The page is untrusted, so it is sandboxed
A published prototype is markup and script made by a model. It is served with a CSP that sandboxes it into its own origin, allows no network calls (`connect-src 'none'`) and no form submissions, allows only pictures, fonts and its own inline code, and with `noindex`, `no-referrer` and a "Prototype · Future Basics" ribbon. The staff preview runs in a sandboxed iframe too.

## Reading other people's sites safely
Only http and https on the normal ports; every address (and every redirect) is resolved and refused if it points at a private, loopback, link-local or metadata address; responses are capped at 1.5 MB and 12 seconds. Staff only.

## Limits and cost
`PROTOTYPES_PER_DAY` (default 25), `PROTOTYPE_CONCURRENCY` (default 2 at a time), 40 builds and 60 audits per person per hour. Each build shows its cost (`PROTOTYPE_PRICE` is the list price per million tokens in and out, default `5,25`). Other settings: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `PROTOTYPE_MODEL`, `BUILDS_PUBLIC_URL` (default `https://builds.thefuturebasics.com`).

## Data and API
Table `prototypes` (`src/db.js`): the facts, audit, brief, quality result, the page, the previous page after a rebuild, tokens and cost, views, and the build it is on. Pure rules: `src/proto-crawl.js`, `src/proto-industry.js`, `src/proto-build.js` (`test/proto.test.js`, `test/proto-build.test.js`). Routes: `src/proto-routes.js` (`/v1/admin/prototypes…`, `/p/:slug`).
