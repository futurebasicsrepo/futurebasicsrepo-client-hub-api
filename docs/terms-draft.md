# Terms of use: draft clauses on copying, scraping and AI

**Status: a draft for a lawyer to review. It is not in force and is not shown to anyone yet.** It covers only what the security work
needs: what people may and may not do with the Design Lab, the hub and what comes out of them. A lawyer should fit it into the full
terms (accounts, payment, liability, governing law) and check it against the places we sell.

The words in *italics* are notes for the lawyer, not part of the clauses.

---

## 1. What is ours, and what is yours

1.1 **Your work is yours.** The designs, pictures, graphics, tech packs and files you make in your room belong to you (or to whoever
you make them for), subject to paying for them where a price applies.

1.2 **The platform is ours.** The Design Lab and the hub belong to Future Basics: the software, its engines (pattern drafting,
footwear lasts, 3D assembly, recipes, fabric checks and the like), the sketch pack, flat templates, masks and parts, the material and
technique libraries, the starting graphics and fonts, the prompts and the way they work together. Using the platform gives you no
right to them beyond what these terms allow.

*Note: check this against what we promise in the pricing page and in contracts (C1/C2). Some packages may transfer more.*

## 2. What you may do

2.1 Use the platform for your own products and your own clients' products.

2.2 Download and share the files your room exports (tech packs, production packages, flats, pattern files, STL/STEP models) with the
people you are making the product with: factories, suppliers, your team, your client.

## 3. What you may not do

3.1 **No copying the platform.** You may not copy, rebuild or imitate the Design Lab or the hub, or any part of them, or help someone
else to.

3.2 **No reverse engineering.** You may not decompile, disassemble, de-obfuscate or otherwise work out how the platform's engines
work, except where the law gives you a right to do that which cannot be taken away.

3.3 **No scraping or bulk downloading.** You may not use bots, scripts, crawlers or anything automated to read, collect or download
from the platform, and you may not pull the libraries (sketches, templates, materials, techniques, graphics) out in bulk, beyond what
you need for the products you are working on.

3.4 **No training or prompting AI on the platform.** You may not use the platform, its libraries, its engines or its outputs to train,
fine-tune, evaluate or prompt an AI model or system meant to do what the platform does, and you may not let anyone else do so. This
does not stop you using AI tools on your own designs.

*Note: we want to keep a customer free to, say, show their own tech pack to an AI assistant. The line is building or improving a rival
tool. Please test the wording against that.*

3.5 **No passing on access.** Your sign-in is yours. Do not share it or sell access to your room. Add your team instead.

3.6 **No getting round the limits.** Do not try to get around sign-in, room boundaries, download limits, or anything else that keeps the
platform safe.

## 4. How we check, and what happens

4.1 **We watch for misuse.** We keep a record of downloads and of how much of each library an account pulls, and every exported file
carries a reference that ties it to the account and room that took it. We use these to keep the platform safe and to enforce these
terms. *Note: this needs a line in the privacy notice too.*

4.2 **Automatic pauses.** If an account pulls far more than ordinary work needs, we may pause that part of the platform for it for a
while. Ask us if you need more.

4.3 **Suspension and termination.** If we reasonably believe an account has broken section 3, we may suspend or close it. We will tell
you why unless the law or the investigation stops us. *Note: refunds and notice periods to be set by the lawyer.*

4.4 **Your files stay yours.** If we close an account, we will give you a reasonable chance to download your own work, unless that would
help the breach.

## 5. Crawlers and AI agents

5.1 Automated agents (including AI crawlers and agents acting for a person) may read only the public pages: the sign-in page, pricing,
start, help and join. Everything else is closed to them, as our robots.txt and page headers say. Using an agent to read the rest is a
breach of 3.3.
