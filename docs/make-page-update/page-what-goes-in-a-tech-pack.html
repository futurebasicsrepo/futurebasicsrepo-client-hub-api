A tech pack is the document a factory makes your product from. It answers every question a sample maker would otherwise email you: what it looks like, what it is made of, how big each size is, how it is sewn, what colour, which labels go where, and how to check it. A factory quotes from it, makes the first sample from it, and checks the bulk order against it.

The pictures on this page are from a real tech pack: a pullover hoodie with a chest pocket and a stone wash, built on our Make page in a minute. You can build your own and download its pack, free.

The cover

The style's name, its category, the sample size, the fit block, the size run and the colourways, beside a picture of the piece. Anyone who picks up the pack should know what they are holding in five seconds.

Technical flats

Flat drawings of the front and the back, in black line, to scale. Not a fashion illustration and not a photo: a flat shows every seam, pocket, zip, rib and stitch line where it really is, so the factory can see what to make. Most delays at sampling start with a flat that leaves something out.

Materials and trims

The bill of materials: the main fabric, every trim (labels, thread, tape, drawcords, eyelets, zips, buttons), what each is, where it goes and how many. If it is on the garment, it is on this list.

Measurements and grading

The points of measure: each measurement named, how to take it, its tolerance, and its value in every size. The sample size is made first; the others are graded from it. A measurement without a "how to measure" gets measured three different ways by three different people.

Construction

How it is built: the seams and how they are finished, the stitches, the pocket and where it sits, the wash and how much it shrinks. Our packs also list the pattern pieces and the result of a check that every seam of the pattern meets its partner, so the first sample is not the first time anyone finds out.

Colourways, labels, packaging and care

Every colourway with its colour reference (Pantone or a lab dip), what each label says and where it sits, how the piece is folded and bagged, and the care instructions. These are the lines most often missing from a first tech pack, and the ones that hold up a shipment at the end.

Sample checks

What to check on the first sample, the fit sample and before the order ships: measure against the pack, compare the colours, count the trims, wash it and measure again. A pack that says how it will be checked gets checked that way.
